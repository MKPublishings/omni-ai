Environment Mode

Mode #12 Planetary Scale v1.0

Environment Mode transforms Ionirix into a full-scope planetary simulation engine. It models Earth across seven spatial scales - from individual villages (1 km²) to the entire planet (510 million km²) - using ten coupled subsystems that exchange data every tick.

Seeded with real-world geography, demographics, climate zones, tectonic plates, and ocean currents, the simulation produces emergent behavior: droughts cascade into migration, trade disruptions ripple through economies, and governance adapts under pressure. Every layer is deterministic and reproducible via configurable RNG seeds.

🔬

Multi-Scale Resolution

Seven discrete scale levels (L0 Planet → L6 Village) with automatic cell generation, cross-scale propagation, and seamless zoom navigation.

🔗

Coupled Subsystems

Ten simulation systems execute in dependency-sorted order each tick. Climate drives hydrology; ecology feeds population; energy powers economy - fully connected.

🌐

Earth Seed Initialization

Pre-loaded with 7 continents, 10 countries, 50+ cities, 16 tectonic plates, 20 climate zones, and 10 ocean currents - all from real-world data.

⏩

Time Acceleration

Run simulations at 1×, 10×, 100×, or 1000× speed. Snapshot and rollback to any prior state. Deterministic replay with identical seeds.

Simulation Subsystems

🌡️

Climate

Atmospheric modeling, seasonal cycles, ENSO oscillation, Koppen zone classification, and stochastic weather event generation.

💧

Hydrology

Water cycle simulation, watershed management, aquifer tracking, ocean currents, tidal modeling, glacier melt, and sea level rise.

🌿

Ecology

Biome distribution, Lotka-Volterra population dynamics, food web modeling, Shannon biodiversity index, carbon sequestration, and deforestation tracking.

⛰️

Geology

Tectonic plate movement, Gutenberg-Richter seismic modeling, volcanic activity with VEI scaling, soil orders, and erosion processes.

🏗️

Infrastructure

Road, rail, airport, seaport, pipeline, power grid, water, waste, and telecom networks with condition-based degradation and maintenance cycles.

👥

Population

Demographics, settlement hierarchy (hamlet to ecumenopolis), migration flows with push/pull factors, disease modeling with R0 transmission.

⚡

Energy

16 energy source types, merit-order dispatch, storage with cycle degradation, energy markets with carbon pricing, and transition state tracking.

📈

Economy

Solow growth model, 20 economic sectors, 28 trade commodities, macroeconomic indicators (GDP, inflation, debt-to-GDP, PMI), and credit ratings.

🏛️

Governance

15 government types, 8 electoral systems, 4-axis political parties, diplomatic relations, treaties, sanctions, military state, and coup risk modeling.

🚢

Transport

24 transport modes, maritime chokepoints (9 real-world), aviation networks, logistics hubs, modal share analysis, congestion, and emissions tracking.
